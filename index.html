The Secret State Banquet

A Global Vocabulary Adventure

🎖️🎻🥧
01

The Big Day

Ben is a government representative. Today is the day of his departure to a foreign country. He didn't hesitate to pack his bags and start this long journey.

02

In the Capital

He arrived at the capital city. At the hotel, the receptionist was very polite. She asked about his identity and checked his official papers to make sure everything was correct.

03

Busy Kitchen

In the kitchen, the preparation for the banquet was crazy! The chef used high quality equipment to cook. He wanted to show that his food was much better than the average meal.

04

The Music Mistake

Ben’s friend, a talented musician, was worried. "My English grammar is bad!" he said. But Ben told him, "Don't worry! Music is like a religion here; everyone loves it. You are making great progress!"

05

Justice is Served

The food was delicious! Everyone in the neighborhood heard the music. In the end, Ben felt that justice was done because all the hard work made the guests very happy.

💡 Learning Tip: Read the orange words out loud and try to use them in your own story!
